Kitchen Climate Control

Restaurant and Food Service HVAC

Restaurants and food-service businesses have climate demands that ordinary equipment can't meet. Kitchens generate enormous heat loads while dining rooms need to stay comfortable, exhaust hoods pull out conditioned air that makeup air units have to replace, and a busy Friday night pushes rooftop units to their limit. Racine HVAC specializes in food-service HVAC, including rooftop package units, split systems, correct tonnage and airflow sizing, makeup air balancing against hood exhaust, and scheduled service built around your hours.

Comfort and air balance are critical issues for any food-service operator. A kitchen running negative pressure pulls dining-room air the wrong way, drags outdoor air through every door, and makes staff miserable during peak service. Our work keeps your system delivering the temperatures and airflow your operation depends on, with documented service records that demonstrate a well-maintained facility to landlords and auditors. We provide detailed reports and service logs after each visit, along with the documentation many commercial leases require.

We work directly with owners, managers, and kitchen staff to plan replacements and maintenance that protect your business and fit your operating schedule. Our technicians are used to working in occupied facilities, installing during off-hours when needed, minimizing disruption, and keeping your kitchen running and your dining room comfortable.

Safe Installation

Professional Installation and Documentation

Commercial property owners have a duty of care to maintain safe, comfortable conditions for employees, customers, and tenants. A poorly installed or neglected HVAC system can result in carbon monoxide hazards, poor indoor air quality, refrigerant leaks, and costly water damage from clogged condensate lines. Racine HVAC provides commercial installations and inspections that cover heat exchanger integrity, proper venting and combustion air, electrical connections and disconnects, refrigerant charge verification, condensate drainage protection, and ventilation rates for occupied spaces. Our written reports identify risks, assign priority levels, and recommend specific preventive actions.

This work serves a dual purpose: it protects people and property, and it provides the documentation that demonstrates due diligence to landlords, insurers, and auditors. Building owners benefit from maintaining regular professional HVAC service and acting on expert recommendations. We maintain detailed service logs, temperature and filter records, and a documentation binder for every account, with more frequent service for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, gyms, and large mechanical rooms.

Rapid Response

Fast Commercial HVAC Service

When a commercial HVAC system fails, the clock starts immediately. Kitchens get too hot to work, shoppers walk out, server rooms overheat, and tenants start complaining. Calls to our commercial line get a technician rolling fast, anywhere in Racine and the surrounding area.

Failed compressors, burned-out condenser fan motors, refrigerant leaks, ignition and gas valve faults, tripped high-limit switches, frozen coils, control board lockouts: our technicians have seen them all on rooftop and split systems. The priority is pinpointing the fault and restoring heating or cooling as quickly as possible. After that, we set up maintenance and monitoring so it doesn't happen again, and each response comes with full documentation for your records.

Preventive Care

Commercial Scheduled Maintenance Programs

The most cost-effective way to manage commercial HVAC equipment is preventing failures before they happen. Racine HVAC offers customized scheduled maintenance programs for commercial properties of all types and sizes. Our programs include scheduled inspections, coil cleaning and condensate line clearing, filter changes with MERV ratings matched to the space, belt and bearing checks, refrigerant charge and static pressure testing, burner and heat exchanger inspection, and seasonal preparation for peak demand throughout the year. Each program is tailored to the property's specific equipment, usage patterns, and budget requirements.

Next to an emergency replacement, scheduled maintenance typically costs a fraction as much, and it sharply cuts the risk of downtime and tenant complaints. Businesses that commit to a program typically see fewer outages, longer equipment life, and lower energy bills. Give us a call to discuss a plan sized to your property and budget.
