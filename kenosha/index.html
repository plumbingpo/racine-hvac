Keeping Your Kenosha HVAC System Running Longer

A little routine care makes all the difference between a system that lasts and one that quits in the middle of the season. Our advice for homeowners is a short list: a new filter every one to three months, two feet of clearance around the outdoor condenser, a gentle rinse each spring for the condenser coil, and a professional tune-up every spring and fall, so problems are found while they're still inexpensive.

Take a look at the age of your system. That number tells you a lot. With regular service, most air conditioners make it to 12 to 15 years, furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15. If your system is past that range and is also showing higher bills, rooms that never quite reach temperature, or repeated repairs, it's usually due for a planned replacement, and that's almost always cheaper and less stressful than an emergency replacement when the first heat wave hits.

The earlier you report a comfort problem, the cheaper it usually is. A capacitor that's starting to fail costs little to replace, yet that same capacitor, ignored through a full season, can ruin the compressor and force a full system replacement. Save our number in your contacts now so help is one call away the moment something seems off in your home. Early intervention saves time, money, and sleepless nights.
